Junsong Wu 吴俊松

I'm an M.S. student in Mechanical Engineering at Shanghai Jiao Tong University, advised by Prof. Zhenhua Xiong. I'm also a research intern at TeleAI, working with Chenjia Bai. Previously, I completed undergraduate studies in the humanities at SJTU.

I aim to build general-purpose intelligence that learns and acts in the physical world. Toward this goal, my research focuses on scaling physical experience through simulation, grounded by real-world interaction. I explore how hardware, simulation, and learning can be co-designed so that physical knowledge accumulates and transfers across tasks, environments, and embodiments.
